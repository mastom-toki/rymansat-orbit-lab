## camera

Create ONE isolated cutout asset for a Japanese family science-event animation. Clear contemporary illustrated science-book style: confident slightly irregular dark pencil outlines, simple opaque gouache colours, readable silhouette and only essential details. Welcoming to children and adults, not babyish, not photorealistic, not glossy toy 3D. No text, logos, faces, scene, floor, shadows outside the object, glow or extra decorations. Genuine transparent alpha background with comfortable margins around the whole object, no checkerboard painting. Subject: one familiar compact digital camera in three-quarter front view, muted charcoal body, large circular glass lens, a single clear silver shutter button on top, small grip. Lens faces toward lower-left, the camera points toward a paper strip placed outside this asset. Show the entire camera with no strap and no flash burst. Broad shapes recognisable at small size.

## satellite

Use case: scientific-educational. A SINGLE CUTOUT ASSET for a friendly Japanese family science-event animation, designed to be immediately recognisable by primary-school children, teenagers, parents and grandparents. Subject: a small generic CubeSat satellite, three-quarter view, compact cubical body with warm gold corner rails and blue solar-cell panels, two short simple solar wings. A clearly visible plain cream rectangular area on the front panel will receive a data-image overlay later; leave it blank. Medium: confident hand-drawn dark graphite outlines with slightly irregular edges and simple opaque gouache fills, like a clear contemporary illustrated science book. Clear strong silhouette, only essential details, moderate natural colours. Welcoming and understandable, neither babyish nor photorealistic. NO eyes/faces, NO tiny technical labels, NO text, NO logos, NO glow, NO scene, NO Earth, NO stars, NO floor, NO background, NO cast shadow beyond the object. Center the ENTIRE satellite with comfortable transparent margins, no cropped antenna or panels. Genuine transparent alpha background, not a checkerboard drawing. This is an explanatory generic satellite, not a specific real spacecraft.
